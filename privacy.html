What happens when you visit

The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel's servers receive the technical details every browser sends to load a page, such as your IP address, the browser you use and the page you asked for. Vercel uses these to deliver the site and keep it safe from abuse, under Vercel's privacy policy. I don't use them to identify anyone.

The site doesn't use analytics, advertising or social media trackers, and the typeface is served from this site rather than a third party.

Cookies and your device

This site sets no cookies. On phones it keeps one small setting in your browser: whether you picked the list or the grid view, so it stays that way next time. It never leaves your device, and you can clear it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Videos

Films are played through YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). Nothing from YouTube loads until you press play. Once a film is playing, YouTube (Google) receives your IP address and may store information on your device, under Google's privacy policy.

If you email me

I use your name, address and message only to reply and to work with you, and keep them for as long as we're in touch or as long as I need them for my records (for example, invoices). I don't share them with anyone or add you to any mailing list.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what information I hold about you, ask me to correct or delete it, or object to how it's used. Just email me. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
